# Community guidelines

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique code and ideas, not people.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, doxxing and deliberate disclosure of
private data are not acceptable.

Use issues for reproducible bugs and scoped proposals. Respect maintainers' time,
do not demand access to private systems, and do not post sensitive material.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful material and restrict participation.
Report concerns privately through an agreed maintainer contact channel; do not
repeat the harmful or private content in public.
